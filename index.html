Who we are

Since 1978, a meeting point for Spanish culture in Sydney

Over the years the Association has promoted Spanish cultural achievements through lectures, concerts and many other social and cultural events.

Amigoss is run by a committee of six: a President, a Treasurer, a Secretary and three committee members. Members elect it every three years at the Annual General Meeting, and it meets monthly at the Amigoss venue.

The organisation holds a charitable fundraising authority under section 13A of the Charitable Fundraising Act 1991.
